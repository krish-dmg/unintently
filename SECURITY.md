## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security and privacy are fundamental pillars of **Unintently v2**. 

Because Unintently operates as a **local-first** application without collecting user credentials, passwords, or personal telemetry:
- Your assignments and private homework notes never leave your device unless you explicitly share or export them.
- The Cloudflare AI integration proxies only the prompt you provide and does not store user profiles.

If you discover a security vulnerability or potential privacy leak:
1. Please **do not** open a public GitHub issue.
2. Email details directly to `krish.7zip@gmail.com` with the subject tag `[SECURITY] Unintently Vulnerability Report`.
3. Provide a clear description and reproducible steps.
4. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and work with you on a coordinated fix.
